The Structural Milestones

Our 6-Step Program

We maintain strict milestones to ensure zero administrative delays.

1

Phase 1: Diagnostic Assessment

Complete analysis of transcripts, research publications, CGPA conversion, and target institution feasibility.

2

Phase 2: Master University Shortlisting

Curating exact program fits mapping program modules to your academic syllabus to maximize admission probability.

3

Phase 3: Creative Document Structuring

Writing, drafting, formatting your critical Statements of Purpose (SOP) and Letters of Recommendation (LOR) without generic templates.

4

Phase 4: Visa Prep & Submission

Navigating the APS, blocked account setups, custom financial proof compilation, and strategic VFS interview mock run.

5

Phase 5: Arrival Setup & Integration

Coordinating city registration steps, WG accommodation searches, and direct student union bank setups prior to landing.

6

Phase 6: Professional Career Strategy

Direct layout of internship search strategies, target local professional networks, and step-by-step career blueprints.
