  <section class="e2g-section" id="e2g-study-pathways" style="background:#f4f0e7">
    <div class="e2g-wrap">
      <div class="e2g-section-head">
        <div><p class="e2g-kicker">After your Bachelor’s</p><h2>Choose a path that matches your ambition.</h2></div>
        <p>Germany offers both research-intensive and application-oriented routes, in public and state-recognised private institutions.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="e2g-pathway-grid">
        <article class="e2g-pathway">
          <div class="e2g-pathway-top"><h3>Master’s degree</h3><span class="e2g-duration">Usually 1.5–2 years</span></div>
          <p>Build specialised expertise, switch into a high-demand field or prepare for research and doctoral work.</p>
          <div class="e2g-steps">
            <div class="e2g-step"><b>1</b><div><h4>Match your academic background</h4><p>Check subject prerequisites, ECTS requirements and programme-specific eligibility.</p></div></div>
            <div class="e2g-step"><b>2</b><div><h4>Select public or private</h4><p>Compare tuition, semester contribution, accreditation, class size, location and industry access.</p></div></div>
            <div class="e2g-step"><b>3</b><div><h4>Prepare the application</h4><p>Degree records, CV, motivation letter, language proof and APS/VPD where applicable.</p></div></div>
          </div>
        </article>
        <article class="e2g-pathway">
          <div class="e2g-pathway-top"><h3>PhD / doctorate</h3><span class="e2g-duration">Often 3–5 years</span></div>
          <p>Pursue independent research through a university, a structured doctoral programme or an industry-linked research position.</p>
          <div class="e2g-steps">
            <div class="e2g-step"><b>1</b><div><h4>Define your research direction</h4><p>Develop a focused topic that aligns with a chair, institute, graduate school or company R&amp;D area.</p></div></div>
            <div class="e2g-step"><b>2</b><div><h4>Find supervision or a position</h4><p>Apply to advertised doctoral roles or approach suitable professors with a strong proposal.</p></div></div>
            <div class="e2g-step"><b>3</b><div><h4>Clarify funding and employment</h4><p>Doctoral candidates may be scholarship-funded, university-employed or employed in collaborative industrial research.</p></div></div>
          </div>
        </article>
      </div>

      <div class="e2g-types">
        <article class="e2g-type"><span class="e2g-type-tag">Universität</span><h3>Research universities</h3><p>Best suited to theoretical depth, academic research and doctoral preparation.</p><ul><li>Broad subject range</li><li>Research-led teaching</li><li>Independent doctorate routes</li></ul></article>
        <article class="e2g-type"><span class="e2g-type-tag">Technische Universität</span><h3>Technical universities</h3><p>Universities with a particularly strong profile in engineering, technology and the natural sciences.</p><ul><li>Advanced laboratories</li><li>Strong industry networks</li><li>Research and innovation focus</li></ul></article>
        <article class="e2g-type"><span class="e2g-type-tag">HAW / Fachhochschule</span><h3>Universities of applied sciences</h3><p>Designed around applied learning, smaller study environments and occupational relevance.</p><ul><li>Projects and internships</li><li>Industry-oriented curricula</li><li>Practice-focused research</li></ul></article>
      </div>
      <div class="e2g-cost-note"><div class="icon">€</div><div><h3>Affordable does not always mean completely free.</h3><p>Most students at state-funded institutions do not pay general tuition, but all students normally pay a semester contribution. Tuition can apply in specific states, programmes and private institutions—always verify the programme page before applying.</p></div></div>
